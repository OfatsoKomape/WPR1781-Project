About Olive & Oak

Where nature meets timeless contemporary design.

Our Story

ESTABLISHED IN 2024

Founded in 2024, Olive & Oak was born out of a shared passion to redefine contemporary spaces across South Africa. We saw an opportunity to bridge the gap between minimalist modern aesthetics and the comforting warmth of natural, organic elements.

What started as a boutique design studio quickly expanded across Pretoria, Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban, and Polokwane. From serene residential sanctuaries like The Langford Loft to landmark biophilic corporate spaces like The Green Quarter, our journey is rooted in creating environments that breathe life, light, and harmony into daily living.

Our Mission

To craft warm, sophisticated, and sustainable interiors that perfectly balance high functionality with organic beauty. We aim to transform everyday spaces into timeless environments that reflect our clients' unique ways of living and working.

What Makes Us Different

We specialise in biophilic contemporary design. Rather than following short lived interior trends, we focus on natural illumination, living architectural features, honed stone, rich walnut woods, and warm metal accents. Every element serves a practical purpose while nurturing human well-being.

Our Core Values

01.Natural Nature look

We prioritise natural, realistic materials from floor to ceiling stone and marble to raw walnut cabinetry and trailing greenery.

02.Loving nature's Well-being

We integrate living walls, glazed skylights, and organic silhouettes that adds creativity, peace, and personal vitality.

03.Timeless Craftsmanship

Every detail, from statement lighting to fixtures, everything is crafted to stand the test of time.
